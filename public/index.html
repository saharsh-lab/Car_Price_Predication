03 · Method

Three steps, one fair price.

A scikit-learn pipeline trained on Indian used-car listings powers every estimate.

i.

Clean the market

Listings are de-duplicated, and units like “23.4 kmpl” or “74 bhp” are parsed into numbers. Missing fuel efficiency is filled with the brand's median.

  • 6,717 usable listings
  • 31 brands, 1994–2020
ii.

Engineer the signal

Domain features capture how buyers actually price a car, beyond the raw specification sheet.

  • Age and kilometres per year
  • Power-to-displacement ratio
  • Luxury-marque flag
iii.

Ensemble the estimate

Three tree ensembles vote on the log of the price, which keeps estimates positive and steady across budget and luxury cars.

  • Histogram gradient boosting · 45%
  • Gradient boosting · 35%
  • Random forest · 20%
